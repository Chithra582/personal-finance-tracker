# Investment Portfolio Tracker Skill

## Overview
Monitors user stock portfolio holdings, calculates cost basis vs. current valuation, and checks asset allocation balances.

## Operations
1. Ingests stocklist with share quantities and buy prices.
2. Computes total portfolio value, realized/unrealized gains, and percentage yields.
3. Evaluates asset concentration risk and recommends diversification balancing.
