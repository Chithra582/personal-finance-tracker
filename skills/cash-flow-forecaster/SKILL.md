# Cash Flow Forecaster Skill

## Overview
Projects forward cash flows over 3, 6, and 12-month horizons based on historical income and recurring expense patterns.

## Operations
1. Aggregates fixed monthly recurring income vs. non-discretionary expenses.
2. Estimates net monthly savings rate.
3. Calculates emergency fund coverage in months of living expenses.
