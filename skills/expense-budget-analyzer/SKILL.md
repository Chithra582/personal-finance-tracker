# Expense Budget Analyzer Skill

## Overview
Analyzes historical debit transactions, classifies entries into standardized expense buckets, and tracks spending against monthly targets.

## Operations
1. Ingests raw transaction streams.
2. Classifies records by category (Housing, Food, Utilities, Transport, Discretionary).
3. Computes monthly burn rate and flags categories approaching budget thresholds.
